Kaggle Dataset & Sanitization

PhishShift utilizes the verified Kaggle Phishing Email corpus, containing real-world benign correspondence, spam campaigns, and targeted spear-phishing lures.

  • Dynamic Column Validation: Detects and validates actual column names (e.g. Email Text, Email Type) dynamically rather than relying on brittle assumptions.
  • Missing Value Elimination: Identified and dropped 16 corrupted rows with null message payloads.
  • Deduplication Pipeline: Detected and purged 1,181 duplicate email messages to prevent test-set leakage and biased class weighting.
  • Sanitized Corpus Size: Cleaned from 18,650 raw records to 17,450 verified instances (10,966 Legitimate, 6,484 Phishing).
  • Stratified Train/Test Split: 80% training (13,960 samples) and 20% test (3,490 samples) with preserved label ratios.

Tri-State Decision Spectrum

Traditional binary classifiers force high-risk decisions on ambiguous inputs. PhishShift implements a calibrated tri-state safety protocol:

P ≥ 65%

Phishing (High Risk)

High probability of malicious intent or multiple urgent heuristic rule breaches. Automated blocking and quarantining recommended.

35% < P < 65%

Uncertain (Flagged for Review)

Statistical uncertainty or model-heuristic conflict. Alerts the user to verify the communication out-of-band before taking action.

P ≤ 35%

Legitimate (Safe)

Statistical alignment with clean business correspondence and zero critical security signature triggers. Normal delivery permitted.

Organizational Defense Recommendations

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Enforce FIDO2/WebAuthn hardware security keys to mitigate credential harvesting attacks even if a user falls for a phishing lure.

DMARC, DKIM & SPF Alignment

Configure strict DMARC rejection policies to prevent domain spoofing and forged sender addresses at the mail server boundary.

Out-of-Band Verification

Establish strict operational protocols requiring direct phone or in-person verification for any fund transfers or credential alterations.

Continuous Security Training

Conduct realistic phishing simulations and empower personnel to report ambiguous emails via one-click reporting tools.
